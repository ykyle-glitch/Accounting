👋 New here? Start in 4 easy steps

  1. Pick your accounting period below: calendar year (Jan–Dec) or your own fiscal year.
  2. Chart of Accounts: check the list of accounts your business uses. Add or remove as needed.
  3. Transactions: record each business event. Total debits must equal total credits.
  4. Trial Balance calculates by itself. Use Adjustments for end-of-period corrections, with a comment explaining why.

Tip: press Load sample data at the top to see a complete example. Everything is saved automatically in this browser.

Adjustments

Adjusting entries fix account balances at the end of the period (for example, supplies used, depreciation, or unpaid salaries). Each one needs a comment explaining why it was made, so anyone reviewing the books understands it.
